Hotspot settings

In the Properties (see Camera settings (explained)) pane, you can specify the settings for the hotspotA particular position for viewing magnified and/or high quality camera images in XProtect Smart Client views.. The Live Stream, Image Quality, Frame Rate, and Maintain Image Aspect Ratio settings apply to all cameras in the hotspot.

To make the properties appear, you must select the view item and then click Setup.
