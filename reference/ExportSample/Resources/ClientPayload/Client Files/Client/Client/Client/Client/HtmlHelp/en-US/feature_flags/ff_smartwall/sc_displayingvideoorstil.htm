Displaying video or still image from bookmark on Smart Wall (explained)

When you send a video clip or still imageA single static image. from a bookmark to a Smart Wall, the bookmark details are displayed in the tile you have selected in the Smart Wall overview. This includes the video clip or the still images, the bookmark heading, the start and end times of day, the moment the bookmark was made, and the user who made it.

You can view bookmark details by hovering the mouse pointer over the name of the camera.
