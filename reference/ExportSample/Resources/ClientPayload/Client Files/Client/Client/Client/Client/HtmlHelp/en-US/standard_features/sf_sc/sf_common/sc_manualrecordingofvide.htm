Record video manually

The functionality of the manual recording feature depends on the surveillance system you are connected to and on your user rights.

Recording while watching live video is useful if you see something of interest.

Steps:

On the camera toolbar for the position in the view you want to record, select:

  • Start recording for # Minutes

    • Once started, recording will continue for the number of minutes determined by your surveillance system administrator. You cannot change this, and you cannot stop recording before the specified number of minutes has passed.

  • Start manual recording

    • Once started, recording will continue for the number of minutes determined by your surveillance system administrator or you can click the icon again to stop manual recording.

    You can start recording the video stream from more than one camera simultaneously, although you must select them one by one.
