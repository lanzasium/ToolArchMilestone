Virtual joystick and PTZ overlay buttons (explained)

If your views include fisheye cameras or lenses, or PTZPan-tilt-zoom; a highly movable and flexible type of camera. devices (see PTZ and fisheye lens images (explained)), you can navigate the images by clicking either the arrow mouse pointer (the virtual joystick) or the PTZ navigation buttons that appear inside the image.
