Welcome

Welcome to the XProtect® Smart Client help.

Some of the images in the help are displayed as thumbnails. Click them to view them in full size.

To search the help:

Use the Search box in the upper-right corner.

To navigate the help:

Desktop computer

Use the table of contents on your left-hand side.

Tablet or smartphone

Use the menu in the upper-left corner.

Navigation buttons

You can use the and buttons to go to the previous or next topic in the table of contents. To navigate according to the browser history, use the Previous and Next buttons in your browser.

Breadcrumb bar

The breadcrumb bar at the top of any topic tells you where you are in the help. Most items in the breadcrumb bar are clickable allowing you to quickly jump to other topics.

To find related topics:

If the Related Topics text appears in a topic, click the text. Other topics related to the subject that you are interested in are displayed in a clickable list.
