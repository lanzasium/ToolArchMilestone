Mask areas in a recording during export

When you export video, you can add privacy masks to cover selected areas. When someone watches the video, the areas with privacy masks appear as solid blocks.

The privacy masks that you add here only apply to the current export and for the selected video. The export may already include video with privacy masks. For more information, see Privacy masking (explained).

Steps:

  1. In the Export window, click the Privacy mask button. A window appears.
  2. Click the button, and then drag the pointer over the area that you want to mask. To mask more areas, repeat this step.
  3. To unmask part of a privacy mask, click the button, and then drag the pointer over the area of the mask that you want to unmask. Repeat this step for each part to unmask.
  4. You can temporarily switch between mask and unmask modes by pressing the CRTL button while you make a selection.
  5. Click OK to return to the Export window.

The preview image contains an invisible grid with cells. If the area you select includes any portion of a cell, the system masks the entire cell. The result can be that the system masks slightly more of the image than you intended.

If you just want to view the image without masks applied, click and hold the Hide mask button. The mask reappears when you release the button.
