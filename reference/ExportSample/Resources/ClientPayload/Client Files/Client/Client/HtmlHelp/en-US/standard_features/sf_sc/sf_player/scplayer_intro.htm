XProtect Smart Client – Player

XProtect Smart Client – Player is a light version of XProtect Smart Client that is included with video and data exported in the XProtect format.

With XProtect Smart Client – Player you can view exported files without having surveillance software installed.

The application has many of the features of XProtect Smart Client and looks similar. However, XProtect Smart Client – Player is only for viewing video exported in the XProtect format.

Do never attempt to open a live database or live archive with XProtect Smart Client – Player, as this may damage the indexing of your recordings, and as a result, they become unavailable.
