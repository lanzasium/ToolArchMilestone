Configure what to show on the timeline tracks

To have the best overview of your recordings, bookmarks, and other markers on the timeline tracks, you can show or hide the information so only what you need is shown.

  1. From the global toolbar, select the Settings and more menu and then Settings.

  2. From the Settings window, select Timeline.

  3. Select if recordings from different devices or sources should be shown or hidden on the timeline tracks. Each type of recording has different colors on the timeline track. You can select to show or hide the following devices or sources:

    • Incoming audio

    • Outgoing audio

    • Additional data - shows metadata coming from other sources

    • Additional markers - shows markers coming from other sources

    • Bookmarks

    • Motion indication - shows recordings with motion

    • All cameras timeline - this timeline track shows all recordings from all cameras in the view.
